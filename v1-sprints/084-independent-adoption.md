# Sprint 084: Independent adoption and repeat use

Status: planned. Mapping: B14 / F4 / E7.
Entry: trial-material preparation can start now; external trials follow F2 interface
revisions and explicit authorization to contact participants. Full GPU completion
is not a prerequisite for a scoped CPU trial.
Shared evidence/closure rules: [roadmap](roadmap-after-063.md).

## Outcome and first check

Learn whether external researchers choose and reuse the foundation for their own
method. First audit the trial packet for a reproducible clean install, an honest
capability boundary and a record that distinguishes self-directed work from help.
An internal author or agent cannot be counted as an external user.

## Work

- Prepare wheels, an install/change/verify/save example, public component docs,
  known limitations and an evidence template. Use participant-defined methods
  and obstacles, not only repository-authored tasks.
- Draft a concrete invitation and trial packet for review. Send nothing before
  the user explicitly authorizes contact; preparation continues independently.
- After authorized contact, observe at least two independent authors. Record actual
  installation, work completed, assistance, blockers, dependency use and reasons
  for continuing or stopping. Preserve refusals and abandonment.
- Seek evidence that one author implements their own method and returns to use it
  on a second task. Record the task/artifact and what was reused. A promise to reuse
  or a successful guided demo is not a repeat-use result.

## Acceptance and reflection

E7 requires two independent authors and the declared own-method/second-task reuse.
Keep not_run or failed adoption evidence explicit even when engineering passes.
Report which benefit mattered: modification effort, correctness, execution or
integration. Downloads/stars, internal agent success and more demos cannot substitute.

Reflect on concrete reasons for adoption or abandonment and propose the next product
investment from that evidence. Fix discovered correctness issues promptly, recording
affected revisions/gates; do not broaden scope merely to count more features.

## Results

Not run. No external contact, independent trial or adoption claim is made here.
